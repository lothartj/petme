WHAT IT DOES

it's not just a wallpaper, it actually lives there

🐕

WALKS & REACTS

Pets idle, walk, sleep, and perk up when you type or move your mouse nearby.

⚽

REAL BALL PHYSICS

Drag the ball and throw it — real gravity, bounce, and rolling friction. Pets chase it and try to catch it.

❤️

CARE & PETTING

Click a pet to pet it. Care decays slowly over time and catches up on relaunch, so don't neglect them too long.

🛍️

THE PET POCKET

A shop to buy new companions, choose which ones are actually out on your desktop, and resize them all.

SCREENSHOT

the HUD, the shop, and a yard full of trouble

HOW THE MONEY WORKS

no ads, no accounts — just use your computer

$ EARNING CASH

You earn 1¢ per second while you're actively using your machine — moving the mouse or typing anywhere counts, not just inside petme. Go idle for 3 seconds and it pauses until you're back. Spend it in the Pet Pocket to grow your collection — prices run from about $120 to $320 per pet, deliberately expensive so it's a real goal.
